Safety & Telemetry

Environmental Telemetry and Micro-Climate Sensing

Integrated mast sensors monitor local atmospheric conditions in real time, delivering automated early warnings to protect event operations.

RS485 Ultrasonic Anemometer

Ultrasonic anemometers measure 3-axis wind speed using sound waves instead of mechanical spinning cups. They resist rain, dust, and icing with no moving mechanical parts.

360-Degree IP67 Mast Cameras

360-degree IP67 cameras provide visual verification and record continuous local logs to solid-state storage, ensuring tamper-proof records of site conditions.

Automated Structural Warnings

Automated warnings for event managers before microbursts damage temporary structures, staging, sound towers, or pop-up vendor canopies.

Tactical Edge Networking & Revenue Protection

Field-Tested Multi-WAN Infrastructure for High-Density Environments

Commercial cellular towers collapse under festival density. We combine Starlink satellite backhaul with dual 5G standalone modems to guarantee point-of-sale continuity.

SpeedFusion™ Hitless Failover

  • Packet-level WAN smoothing and hot failover.
  • Sub-3-second link cutover across satellite and dual cellular lines with zero payment session resets.
  • 99.999% Point-of-Sale uptime SLA under 30,000+ attendee crowd congestion.

Hard-Walled POS Isolation (PCI-DSS Ready)

  • Dedicated IEEE 802.1Q VLAN 10 segmentation with DSCP 46 Expedited Forwarding QoS rules.
  • Merchant card readers are isolated from attendee Wi-Fi and edge computing traffic.
  • End-to-end 256-bit AES transport encryption to private termination endpoints.

Zero-Trust Remote Diagnostics (InControl 2 & InTouch)

  • Out-of-band fleet orchestration and live cellular RF telemetry (RSRP, RSRQ, SINR).
  • Direct remote LAN access to on-site card terminals, IP cameras, and power controllers via Peplink InTouch without opening public firewall ports.
